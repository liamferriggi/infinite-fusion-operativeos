# Market Research Summary

## Categories

The market splits into three categories.

## SOP Documentation Tools

Examples:

- Tango
- Scribe
- Guidde
- Trupeer
- Glyde

These tools record screen workflows and produce guides or SOPs. They are strong for documentation and training, but most do not execute the workflow as an agent.

## RPA and Process Mining

Examples:

- UiPath Task Capture / Task Mining
- Microsoft Power Automate
- Automation Anywhere Process Discovery

These tools are mature and enterprise-oriented. They can discover processes and automate workflows, but are often heavy, expensive, and require specialists.

## AI Workflow-to-Agent Tools

Examples discovered during research:

- SkillForge
- Latch
- AgentPaths

These are closer to the product vision: record a workflow, produce structured process knowledge, and create agent-ready instructions. This category is newer and still proving reliability.

## Opportunity

The strongest opening is not a generic recorder.

The opening is:

> Vertical workflow capture plus supervised execution.

The product should not compete head-on with UiPath or generic SOP tools. It should pick a narrow industry and automate high-frequency admin workflows end to end.

## Positioning

Recommended positioning:

> Record your best operator once. Infinite Fusion turns the workflow into a supervised AI worker that handles repetitive admin tasks and asks for approval on exceptions.

Avoid:

> Replace all staff automatically.

The safer and more credible message is supervised work reduction first.

## Validation Criteria

A workflow is a strong target if:

- It happens daily or weekly.
- It takes 10 or more minutes manually.
- It touches a portal, CRM, or admin system.
- It has clear rules but messy exceptions.
- It has measurable cost or time savings.
- The owner would pay at least `$500/month` to reduce the work.
